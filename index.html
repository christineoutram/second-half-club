What we mean by “the second half”

There is no magic birthday.

For us, the second half begins around 40 — not because life suddenly changes on your birthday, but because this is often when a lot starts shifting at once.

Bodies change. Perimenopause and menopause enter the conversation. Careers evolve. Friendships move around. Parents get older. Relationships change shape. Money means something different. And plenty of women start asking a deceptively simple question: what do I want now?

The Second Half Club is a place for women 40+ to explore all of it: midlife health, changing bodies, work, money, friendship, sex, travel, identity, reinvention and the practical business of building a life that still feels like yours.
